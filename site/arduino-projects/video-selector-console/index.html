Video Selector Console

A console for switching ensemble and recording room in real time.

In my 5th semester at Metropolitan College, a team of four of us was formed for the Public Project course. We decided to research how the acoustics of a room affect a recording, so we recorded 3 different ensembles in 7 different rooms. While preparing the presentation, I built this console.

The idea is that each listener picks the ensemble they want to hear with one of the 3 buttons in the top row, and then, while the piece is playing, switches in real time between the rooms it was recorded in.

The console uses 10 illuminated push buttons and 2 Arduino Uno boards.
